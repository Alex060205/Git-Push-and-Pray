# Vitala

Living with chronic pain and central sensitization already takes a lot of energy. Keeping track of exercise instructions, symptoms and when to adjust a routine shouldn’t add more work.

Built specifically to support existing doctor-patient workflows for graded exercise in chronic pain care, Vitala bridges the gap between clinical guidance and everyday life. Vitala interprets, organizes, and tracks clinician-prescribed exercise plans, turning them into a simple, guided experience patients can follow with just a few clicks. 

Less cognitive load. Less confusion. Easier follow-through. Ultimately, better patient outcomes and quality of life.
 

Built for **natHacks 2026** by the **Git Push and Pray** team.

## The idea

Currently, when a care team prescribes a graded exercise regimen for a patient with chronic pain and central sensitization, much of the responsibility falls on the patient. They're expected to interpret their care plan, understand exercise instructions and progressions, monitor their pain levels, track their progress, and document their experiences to share at future appointments.

For patients already dealing with the cognitive and physical demands of chronic pain, this can quickly become overwhelming. Important information gets missed, documentation becomes another chore, and the effort required to manage the care plan can get in the way of actually following it.

Vitala aims to change that.

Acting as a companion between appointments, Vitala takes the instructions provided by the care team and turns them into a simple, guided experience. Instead of having to interpret and organize everything themselves, patients can open the app, find their prescribed exercises, follow the instructions, and answer the questions specified by their clinician. Vitala takes care of organizing and documenting their responses and progress, keeping everything in one accessible place.

We also want to make remembering easier. With calendar integration and timely reminders, Vitala can help patients fit their prescribed exercises into their daily routines without having to constantly remember when they're supposed to do them.

When it's time for their next appointment, patients can generate a summary of their progress and experiences to bring back to their care team, helping make those conversations more informed and productive.

The goal is simple: take the work out of managing the care plan, so patients can focus on following it. Less time interpreting, organizing, and documenting. More energy for living

## What we built

- Prescription reading from photos, PDFs, and typed text.
- Exercise adjustments based on the care team’s rules and the patient’s reports.
- Pain, recovery and exercise tracking.
- Daily and weekly check-ins.
- Reminders and activity history.
- PDF reports to support follow-up conversations.

## How to access it

Vitala currently runs on Android.

1. Open our [Releases page](https://github.com/Alex060205/Git-Push-and-Pray/releases).
2. Download `app-debug.apk` from the demo release.
3. Open it on your Android phone and allow installation from that source if prompted.
4. Launch Vitala and choose **Use demo user (Maria Lutke)** to explore the app.

The demo uses fictional patient records. Prescription reading requires
internet access and a configured Gemini API key.

You can also build the app yourself by opening this repository in Android Studio.
