# Vitala

Living with chronic pain already takes a lot of energy. Keeping track of exercise instructions, symptoms and when to adjust a routine shouldn’t add more work.

Vitala is an app that helps patients follow a clinician’s exercise plan, one session at a time.

Built for **natHacks 2026** by the **Git Push and Pray** team.

## The idea

A clinician gives a patient an exercise plan. The patient follows it, records how they feel and checks what to do next.

Vitala handles the comparisons and calculations behind that next step. Instead of asking patients to work through a page of instructions every day, we want to give them a clear exercise target and a short explanation.

## What we built

- Prescription reading from photos, PDFs, and typed text.
- Exercise adjustments based on the clinician’s rules and the patient’s reports.
- Pain, recovery and exercise tracking.
- Daily and weekly check-ins.
- Reminders and activity history.
- PDF reports to support follow-up conversations.
